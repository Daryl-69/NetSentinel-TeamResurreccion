The global figure is a between-host effect. Red-team targets are the busiest machines, so the model ranks which host is unusual, not when it was attacked. Remove that axis and the signal is chance. We built this check ourselves, ran it, and it argues against us — across 3 seeds, so it is a measurement, not noise.

The claim we can defend

On 13 days of real enterprise traffic the Sentry recovers 96.9% of everything the Inspector would flag, at 13.7× fewer parameters on CPU. Against the real red team the Inspector is at chance within-host (0.557) — the cascade faithfully compresses a teacher that does not separate credential-based lateral movement, and we say so. The 96.9% is agreement with the teacher: an efficiency number, never a detection rate.
